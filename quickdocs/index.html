What a rewrite looks like

The example below is constructed for illustration. It is not a real client and not a sample of past work — QuickDocs has no past clients yet, and inventing one would be dishonest.

Before:

Welcome to our website! We are a family owned company committed to excellence and quality service. Contact us today to learn more about what we can do for you.

After:

Emergency plumbing across the North Shore, seven days a week
Family-run since 1998. Licensed and insured. Most call-outs reached the same day.

  • Burst pipes, blocked drains, failed water heaters
  • Fixed quote before any work starts
  • Serving Beverly, Salem and Danvers

Every specific in an "after" comes from facts the business already publishes about itself. Which brings us to the rule below.

What QuickDocs will not do

  • Invent facts about your business. No made-up years in business, certifications, guarantees, prices, awards or statistics. If a claim cannot be traced to something you publish or tell us, it does not go in your copy.
  • Write reviews, testimonials or ratings. Those have to come from real customers.
  • Take on work that cannot be finished within 72 hours.
